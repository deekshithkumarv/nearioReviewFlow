Questions owners ask

Do you only send happy customers to Google?

No. That is review gating and Google does not allow it. Every customer sees the same Google review option after sending feedback.

Does Neario write reviews for my customers?

No. Customers write and post their own Google reviews. The tool can help tidy grammar in a customer’s own words, but never changes what they meant.

How does yearly billing work?

You pay one bill of ₹5,391, which covers 9 months at ₹599. The first 3 months are free, so you use Neario for the full 12 months.

Do I pay GST on top?

No. The price you see is the price you pay. No GST is added right now.

Can I get a refund on the yearly plan?

Yes, if you ask within 7 days of paying. We refund the full ₹5,391. After 7 days the yearly payment is not refundable.

Do my customers need to install an app?

No. They scan a QR code or tap a stand and the page opens in their browser.

What is a TapReview device?

A small stand that opens the same feedback page as the QR code when a phone is tapped on it.

Can the replies post automatically?

You can approve each reply yourself. Automatic replies are optional and come with limits you set, such as holding low-rated reviews for your approval.

Which languages can customers use?

The feedback page is available in English, Hindi and Kannada.
