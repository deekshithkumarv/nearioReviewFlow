Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Neario ReviewFlow helps local businesses collect feedback from their customers. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can have it removed.

Two kinds of people

Customers leave feedback for a business. Business owners use Neario to read that feedback. This page covers both.

What a customer shares

  • A star rating, the service they picked, and any comment they type.
  • Which location they visited, and whether they scanned a QR code or tapped a device.
  • A phone number, only if they type one in and tick the box that lets the business contact them.

We do not ask customers to create an account.

Why we use it

  • To show the feedback to the business that was reviewed, so it can improve.
  • To create the business's charts, trends and suggestions.
  • To contact the customer about their feedback, only if they gave a number and agreed.

We do not sell customer feedback or phone numbers.

Google reviews are separate

Feedback sent through Neario is private feedback. It is not a Google review. After sending feedback, every customer sees the same option to write a Google review, whatever their rating. Customers write and post Google reviews themselves, on Google.

What a business owner shares

Name, email, business details, services, locations, and a Google Business Profile connection if the owner chooses to connect one. Billing details are handled by our payment provider.

How long we keep it

Deleting your data

Customers: to delete your feedback or phone number, ask the business you left it for, or write to us at [add your support email] with the business name and the date. You can also withdraw your permission to be contacted the same way.

Business owners: write to [add your support email] to close your account and delete its data.

Who can see it

  • The business that received the feedback, and its team members.
  • The small set of service providers that run Neario for us, such as hosting and payments.
  • Anyone the law requires us to share it with.

Security

We use encrypted connections and limit who on our team can open client data. No online service can promise perfect security.
